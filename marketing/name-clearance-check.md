## Practical conclusion

The name "Dream Decor Studio Iceland" looks low-risk based on the public Icelandic trademark and company-register checks performed.

Main caveat:

- "Dream" and "Decor" are descriptive/common words, so the name may be less distinctive as a protectable trademark unless the full combination and logo are used consistently.

Recommended next step before final launch:

- Ask ISIPO or an Icelandic IP professional for a formal preliminary trademark search before printing final business cards, spending heavily on ads, or registering the mark.
