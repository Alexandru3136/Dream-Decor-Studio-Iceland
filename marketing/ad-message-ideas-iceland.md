# Dream Decor Studio Iceland - Ad Message Ideas

Market: Iceland
Languages: English first, Icelandic drafts for review
Tone: elegant, warm, refined, practical

## Big Positioning

Dream Decor Studio Iceland should feel like:

- elegant event styling without visual noise;
- warm, candlelit atmosphere for meaningful celebrations;
- tailored decor for Icelandic venues, homes, restaurants, and intimate spaces;
- a premium but approachable service.

Core message:

Your date, place, and mood. We shape the decor around it.

## Photo And Video Shot List For Client

Ask the client to capture:

- wide shot of full table setup;
- close-up of flowers;
- close-up of candles;
- invitation/card/menu detail;
- napkin and plate setting;
- before/after venue corner;
- hands placing candles or flowers;
- vertical video walking around the table;
- vertical video lighting candles;
- detail of logo/business card near decor;
- seasonal entrance or window decor;
- photo corner setup;
- ceremony arch or backdrop;
- empty space before styling;
- final space with warm lights on.

## What To Avoid

- too many stock-like smiling people;
- messy backgrounds;
- over-saturated colors;
- heavy red/green Christmas look unless the client wants it;
- generic luxury without Icelandic warmth;
- text-heavy ad images;
- promising exact prices before pricing is ready.
